## In short

Dokan Hisab keeps your shop's records **on your phone only**. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.
The app has no account, no login, no advertising and no analytics, and it cannot connect to the internet.

## What the app stores

What you type into the app: your shop's name, address and phone number; products, prices and stock; customers'
names and phone numbers; sales, dues, payments and expenses; warranty notes; your settings (language, currency).
This is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We never see it.

## Internet and third parties

The app does not have the internet permission and contains no code from advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or
other third-party services. Nothing is sent from the app to us or to anyone else by the app itself.

## Backups and files you make or share

- **Backup files.** When you make a backup, the app creates a file with all the data of one shop, including customer
  names and phone numbers. It is not encrypted. You decide where it goes (your phone, a memory card, email, a cloud
  drive). Anyone who gets the file can read it. Keep it safe and send it only to people you trust.
- **PDF reports and receipts.** These can contain customer names, phone numbers and money amounts. The app shows
  them or hands them to the Android share sheet only when you ask. What happens after that depends on the app you
  choose (for example email), and is covered by that app's own policy.
- **Contacts.** The app does not read your contacts. If you choose a contact for a customer, Android gives the app only
  that one contact's name and number.

## Android automatic backup

Android can back up some app data to **your own Google account** and can copy it to a new phone. Dokan Hisab allows
this for its shop databases and a small settings file. This is done by Android and Google under your Google account
settings and Google's privacy policy; we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off or delete it in Android Settings,
Google, Backup.

## Children

The app is made for shop owners and is not meant for children.

## Deleting your data

- Delete one shop inside the app (Shops, Delete). It is removed from the phone for good.
- Uninstall the app, or Android Settings, Apps, Dokan Hisab, Storage, Clear storage, to remove everything.
- Delete backup files and PDFs you made where you saved or sent them.
- Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.
