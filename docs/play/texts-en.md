## Full description (limit 4000 characters)

```
Dokan Hisab is a simple notebook for a small shop, on your phone. It keeps your sales, your customers' dues (khata), your stock and your expenses, in Bengali or English.

Everything stays on your phone. The app has no account, no login and no internet permission, so it works fully without internet.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Sell: pick products, enter the quantity, add a discount or delivery charge, take cash or write the rest as due. You can enter a sale of an earlier day (back date).
• Warranty: note the warranty period of a product when you sell it, and it is printed on the receipt.
• Receipts: show a receipt, or share it as a text message with the customer.
• Products and stock: add products with price and unit, see what is running low, add stock when new goods arrive.
• Customers and khata: see who owes you how much, write down payments, see each customer's history.
• Expenses: write down your daily costs.
• Reports: sales, dues, costs and profit for one day, a week, a month, a year or any range of dates. Export a report as PDF.
• Several shops: keep more than one shop in one app. Each shop has its own products, customers and sales.
• Backup and restore: make a backup file and keep it in a safe place (for example in your email or on a memory card). If you change phones, restore it and carry on. The app tells you when you have not made a backup for a while.
• Bengali and English: change the language and the currency sign in Settings.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Your data is on your phone only. If you lose the phone or uninstall the app and have no backup file, we cannot get your data back. Please make backups.
• The PDF reports and backup files contain customer names, phone numbers and money amounts. Send them only to people you trust.
• This is a record-keeping tool. It does not take payments and it does not connect to a bank or to any online service.

Questions or problems: see the contact address on this page.
```
